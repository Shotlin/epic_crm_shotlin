# Epic BOS Product Charter

## Mission

Build the most capable, coherent, and efficient business management system possible: an Electron-first operating system that reaches functional parity with AureusERP, ERPNext, and Odoo, then exceeds them in usability, automation, analytics, extensibility, security, and integration quality.

## Non-negotiable scope

Epic BOS will cover the complete business lifecycle:

- Company, branch, identity, authorization, audit, workflows, approvals, localization, currencies, fiscal controls, extensibility, migrations, backup, and recovery.
- Party master, CRM, sales, subscriptions, pricing, procurement, inventory, warehouse, manufacturing, quality, maintenance, projects, services, support, field service, HR, payroll, finance, treasury, tax, consolidation, planning, and reporting.
- Imports, exports, APIs, webhooks, email, calendars, documents, payments, shipping, e-commerce, marketplaces, banking, collaboration, automation, analytics, AI assistance, and ecosystem integrations.

## Product rules

1. Feature parity is tracked explicitly; no major capability from the three reference products is silently omitted.
2. Modules share one business kernel for identity, permissions, audit, workflow, money, documents, events, and integrations.
3. Security defaults to deny. Sensitive actions require explicit permissions, company scope, audit evidence, and segregation-of-duties checks.
4. Financial and operational records are deterministic, versioned, traceable, reversible through controlled corrections, and safe under concurrent use.
5. Extensibility is a first-class feature: custom fields, workflows, policies, views, reports, automations, and integrations must not require forks.
6. Desktop, keyboard, accessibility, responsiveness, offline resilience, and operational clarity are product requirements—not cosmetic additions.
7. AI may recommend, explain, classify, forecast, and automate, but consequential actions remain permissioned, explainable, reviewable, and audited.
8. Every execution phase remains runnable, tested, migratable, observable, backup-capable, and packageable.

## Competitive standard

Epic BOS will not win by accumulating disconnected screens. It will win by combining broad domain depth with a cleaner information model, faster workflows, superior cross-module intelligence, dependable controls, and a consistent operator experience.
